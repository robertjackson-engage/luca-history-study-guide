CHANGES

Early: enclosure movement · Agricultural Revolution (start of the IR) · urbanization · water powered factories.

Later: steam engine powered factories · assembly lines (more efficient) · germ theory (improved sanitation).

IMPACT

Positive: suffrage movement · child labor laws · inventions (steam engine, cotton gin).

Negative: poor living and working conditions · pollution · child labor · tenements · cholera.

BIG IDEAS

Capitalism: private owners compete for profit (Smith).

Laissez-faire: government leaves business alone.

Socialism: society or government owns major industries; wealth shared more equally.

Communism: all property owned in common, no classes (Marx).

Suffrage: the right to vote.
